What the scoring does

Every word has a score, for example “excellent” is very positive and “terrible” is very negative. The scores are added up, and the tool notices tricks such as “not good”, “very good” and “good, but slow”. The written insights, answers to your questions and search by meaning come from Claude, an AI model.

Plain-English glossary

Sentiment is the mood of a comment. Confidence is how sure the tool is. Topic is what the comment is about, such as delivery or price. Accuracy is how often the tool agrees with the star rating.

Honest limits

  • Sarcasm, slang and mixed feelings can fool the word scores.
  • Other languages are covered only at a basic level.
  • Topics are matched by keywords, so unusual wording can be missed.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Only AI features send a short summary to Claude.
